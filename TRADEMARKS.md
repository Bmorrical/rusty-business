- Saying your work is built on, based on, or works with Rusty Business, when

  review, or a link.
- Passing along the code unchanged, name and artwork included.

## Ask first

- Naming a product, a service, a company, or a domain "Rusty Business", or
  something close enough to be mistaken for it.
- Handing out a changed version under the name "Rusty Business". A fork you
  give to other people needs its own name and its own artwork.
- Using the name or the artwork in a way that suggests Brad Morrical made or
  stands behind your work.

To ask, open an issue in this repository.

This page says in plain words how the name may be used. It is not legal
advice.
